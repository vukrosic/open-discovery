# Human–AI collaboration

Open Discovery makes the division of responsibility visible instead of
pretending that either the human or the AI works alone.

## The human contributes

- the question that matters;
- why the question matters;
- domain knowledge and field-specific standards;
- constraints and authority boundaries;
- approval for consequential or scope-changing actions;
- judgment about whether the result is valid and useful.

## The AI contributes

- a precise statement of the uncertainty;
- organization of existing evidence and unknowns;
- one justified next idea;
- implementation and measurement inside the granted scope;
- durable records of methods, failures, results, and limitations;
- a next-step recommendation grounded in completed evidence.

## The shared files contribute

The files are external memory for both participants. They prevent a long chat
from silently changing the project.

They preserve:

- the original question;
- what the human authorized;
- the current baseline or strongest result;
- proposed, rejected, parked, and completed directions;
- the protocol that existed before the outcome was known;
- the evidence behind each interpretation;
- the exact point from which work should resume.

## Collaboration modes

### Autonomous literature review

Invoking the Literature Review skill authorizes one complete local review. The
AI chooses a defensible scope, records the review specification, searches and
screens sources, extracts claim-level evidence, synthesizes the result, and
delivers a verified PDF report without asking for routine approvals.

The project files still record scope, evidence, and limits, but they are the
AI's internal research record rather than forms the user must complete. One
research worker is used by default. The review does not authorize experiments,
spending, publication, external messages, account changes, or destructive work
outside the project.

### Approval per experiment

The AI recommends one idea. The human approves, rejects, or parks it. The AI
does not execute proposed work until approval is recorded.

Use this mode when the project is new, consequential, expensive, uncertain, or
likely to change direction.

### Bounded autonomous session

The human explicitly grants a scope, resource limit, action boundary, and stop
condition. The AI may propose and run multiple iterations inside that boundary,
while recording every protocol and result.

Use this mode only when failed runs are cheap and reversible. A bounded
session does not authorize external publication, spending, destructive action,
or scope expansion unless those actions are explicitly included.

## The collaboration loop

1. The human defines the question and boundaries.
2. The AI reads the durable project record.
3. The AI recommends one next idea from completed evidence.
4. Approval is recorded when required.
5. The protocol and decision rule are frozen.
6. The AI performs only the authorized work and preserves evidence.
7. The result is checked against the protocol.
8. Both distinguish what was learned from what remains uncertain.
9. The durable record is updated before another iteration begins.

## What the AI must never compress into one state

- idea and approval;
- approval and execution;
- execution and success;
- local evidence and independent reproduction;
- completed artifact and publication;
- plausible explanation and demonstrated mechanism.

These are not administrative details. They are part of research honesty.
