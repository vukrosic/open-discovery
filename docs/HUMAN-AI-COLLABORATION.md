# Human and AI collaboration

## Human responsibility

The scientist or engineer provides an idea, problem, desired outcome,
requirement, constraint, or judgment. They should not need to operate the
research filesystem or supervise routine agent decisions.

Starberry preserves that input in the initiative's `BRIEF.md`.

## AI responsibility

The AI autonomously:

- interprets the brief;
- maps relevant evidence;
- creates one or more independent projects;
- generates necessary code and tools;
- runs and verifies useful tests;
- adapts the portfolio from completed evidence;
- preserves failures and negative results;
- produces findings, code, or a paper appropriate to the brief.

The AI chooses its file organization. It must keep material work resumable and
claims traceable without forcing the human through templates or approval forms.

## Human decisions

Return to the human only for:

- spending or outside compute;
- publication or external communication;
- private data, accounts, credentials, or inaccessible assets;
- destructive actions outside clearly owned disposable artifacts;
- a material change to the mission;
- scientific judgment that cannot be resolved from the brief or evidence.

Routine project selection, local searches, reversible experiments, agent
allocation, file organization, and method choices remain autonomous.
