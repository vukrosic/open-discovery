# Biology mode

Use this mode for biological mechanisms, bioinformatics, genomics, molecular
and cellular systems, ecology, evolution, and biomedical research questions.

## Field-specific tools

- **System definition:** record the organism, tissue, cell type, condition,
  scale, and biological endpoint.
- **Evidence hierarchy:** distinguish primary experiments, datasets, reviews,
  databases, preprints, and mechanistic interpretation.
- **Dataset and assay ledger:** preserve accession, version, sample definition,
  measurement method, preprocessing, and exclusions.
- **Replicate and batch check:** separate biological replicates from technical
  replicates and inspect batch effects.
- **Analysis-plan freezer:** define comparisons, covariates, statistical tests,
  multiple-testing treatment, and exclusion rules before analysis.
- **Alternative-mechanism table:** list competing explanations and the
  observation that would distinguish them.
- **External validation check:** test another cohort, dataset, organism,
  condition, or measurement method when the claim requires transfer.
- **Figure-provenance check:** connect every plotted value to its source data
  and analysis step.

## Minimum evidence for a result

Record provenance, biological context, sample size, unit of analysis,
replicates, controls, preprocessing, exclusions, uncertainty, and limitations.
Do not turn association into mechanism or a computational prediction into an
experimental biological finding.

## Useful first experiments

- reproduce one published analysis from an accessible dataset;
- test whether a claimed signal survives batch and covariate checks;
- compare competing mechanisms against one discriminating observation;
- run a bounded literature review to locate the strongest unresolved evidence
  gap before proposing new data collection.
