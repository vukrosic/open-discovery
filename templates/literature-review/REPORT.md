# Literature review report: [Title]

## Executive summary

State the narrowest useful conclusion, the evidence strength, and the most
important limitation.

## Review question

State the exact question and why it matters.

## Scope and method

Summarize the review type, dates, languages, source types, databases or sites,
queries, screening approach, coverage, and stopping rule. Link to
`REVIEW-SPEC.md` and `SEARCH-LOG.md` for the complete record.

## Findings

Organize findings by question or theme. Cite source IDs from
`EVIDENCE-TABLE.md` and distinguish direct evidence from interpretation.

## Conflicting evidence

Explain meaningful disagreements, methodological differences, and uncertainty.

## Evidence gaps

State what was unsupported, inaccessible, or still unknown. Possible future
research may be described but is not executed by this review.

## Limitations

Describe search, access, language, date, screening, extraction, and transfer
limits. Do not claim exhaustiveness unless the frozen method warrants it.

## Conclusion

Give the decision-relevant conclusion without extending beyond the reviewed
evidence.

## References

List every cited source with a full citation and stable URL, DOI, archive ID,
or equivalent identifier.
