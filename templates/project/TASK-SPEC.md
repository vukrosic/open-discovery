## Evidence contract

- Preserve raw evidence and negative results.
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Record versions, provenance, environment, assumptions, and deviations.
- Do not weaken a gate after seeing the outcome.
- Do not convert a profile or partial run into a broader claim.

- Run one bounded direction at a time.
- Stop at the first frozen failure gate.
- After two stale iterations, change mechanism.
